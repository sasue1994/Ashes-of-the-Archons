Why play Ashes of the Archons

Classic RTS in the spirit of Red Alert 2 that loads in seconds in your browser, with new mechanics that make every match a fight over the map.

Browser game, nothing to install

Open the link and play. No files to download, no account. Jump in as a Guest right away.

Seize ruins, steal blueprints

Three tiers of Archon ruins awaken over time. Decrypt them to unlock new weapons and fight over Data Cores in the middle of the battlefield.

Orbital Strike superweapon

Take the central Citadel to unlock a beam from orbit that can turn the game in one shot. The enemy wants it too.

3 AI difficulty levels

From first-time RTS player to veteran. The AI uses the same units as you and never cheats; it differs in speed, army size and tactics.

PC or mobile

Full mouse and hotkey RTS controls, or tap, drag and pinch-zoom on touch screens. Built to be genuinely playable on a phone in landscape.

Low-poly 3D

Classic angled RTS camera with an optional toon style. Light enough to run smoothly on everyday devices.

How to play in 3 steps

A match lasts about 15–25 minutes. Destroy the enemy Construction Yard before they destroy yours.

  1. Build and earn

    Build a Refinery, send Harvesters to collect ore, then add Barracks, a War Factory and a Lab.

  2. Seize the ruins

    Clear the guardians, drive a Decryption Rig into the ring, and carry the Data Core home to research new weapons.

  3. Destroy the enemy

    Mass upgraded tanks and infantry, attack-move into the enemy base and take down their Construction Yard.

Pick your difficulty

Every level uses units as strong as yours. No cheating.

Easy

No base attacks for 7 minutes, slow to react, no Rig hunting, no research. Great for learning RTS.

Normal

No base attacks for 4 minutes, fights for ruins, blocks decryption, hunts nearby Rigs and researches upgrades.

Hard

Attacks from minute 2, fast, big bonus income, hunts Rigs map-wide. For players who want a real challenge.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ashes of the Archons free?

Yes, completely free. No ads and no in-game purchases. If you enjoy it you can optionally support the developer via PromptPay.

Do I need to download anything or sign up?

No. Open the page and press play. You join as a Guest and your name is stored only in that browser.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, on phones and tablets in landscape. The game has touch controls: tap to select and command units, use two fingers to pan and zoom the map.

Is it like Red Alert?

It is a real-time strategy game inspired by Red Alert 2: harvest ore, build a base, train infantry and tanks, and destroy the enemy Construction Yard. It adds its own twist with ancient ruins and weapon blueprints.

How long is a match?

About 15–25 minutes, depending on the AI difficulty and your play style.
